APOCALYPSE: LAST STREET

Open this project with Unity 6000.0.56f1. Open Assets/Scenes/HalloweenCity.unity and press Play.
Choose Play Game, then Easy, Medium or Hard. Main Menu contains Stats, Controls, How to Play, Settings and Credits.

Default controls
W/A/S/D: move on the street. Left Shift: sprint. Space: jump.
Left mouse: walk and shoot (sprinting is disabled while shooting).
Q: switch rifle / laser when laser ammunition is available.
F: aim a saved airstrike. Left click: confirm. Right click: cancel.
Escape or Pause: pause / resume. Controls supports saved keyboard rebinding.
During the final fight, aim at the boss with the mouse. The camera keeps a fixed street-facing angle.

Campaign
Cross checkpoint 1 at z=175, checkpoint 2 at z=353 and checkpoint 3 at z=480.
Crossing saves the next level, position, health, points, laser cells and airstrikes without teleporting the player.
Death -> Continue checkpoint reloads that saved checkpoint. New game starts a new campaign.
After level three, the Boss Monster fights alone: no zombie spawning, rolling pumpkins or hostile missiles.
The boss has a world-space health bar above its head. The player health and points are at the top of the HUD.
Victory follows the boss defeat. Maximum stored airstrikes: three. Laser pickup: 40 cells, capacity 160.
Green heals 35 health. Cyan grants laser cells. Purple grants an airstrike.
Male kills: 10 points. Female kills: 25 points. Checkpoints: 250 / 500 / 750. Boss: 2000.

Hazards and difficulty
Red circles brighten for three seconds before a missile descends. Hostile blast contact is lethal.
Orange street lines mark a giant rolling pumpkin's crossing. Pumpkin contact is lethal.
Move away from these ground warnings. The slower Boss Monster pursues from behind during the street levels.
Easy reduces population, speed and health. Medium is the previous baseline. Hard adds pressure with capped speeds.
Males are more frequent and slower/weaker than females. Zombie spawn positions are randomized ahead of the player.
Street bounds prevent leaving the route. In the final battle, the arena spans z=460..532.

Audio and presentation
Pause -> Settings changes game master/music/effects volume and reduced muzzle flashes.
Soundtrack: Lost in a Bad Place by congusbongus (CC0).
Fonts: Bebas Neue / Rajdhani (SIL OFL). Credits and license files are included under Assets.
Menu art is original generated artwork. See Assets/StreamingAssets/ThirdPartyNotices.txt.

Project organization
All active C# files are in Assets/Scripts, separated by Player, Combat, Enemies, Game, Animation, Camera, Effects and Editor.
Assembly-CSharp.csproj and kingsley_jermain_project1.sln are regenerated for Visual Studio.
No paid assets were purchased. Removed content is recoverable outside this project under the Codex task's outputs/RemovedProjectContent-20261026.
Keep Assets, Packages, ProjectSettings and UserSettings. Library is Unity's generated cache.
